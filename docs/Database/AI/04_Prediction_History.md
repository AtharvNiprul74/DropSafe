# Table - prediction_history

**Schema:** AI

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** DRAFT

---

# Purpose

The `prediction_history` table stores the historical record of AI predictions generated for Students.

Every Prediction generated by the AI model is permanently preserved in Prediction History.

Prediction History enables trend analysis, model comparison, auditing, and historical reporting.

Prediction History is immutable.

---

# Aggregate Owner

AI Aggregate

---

# Template Type

Historical Entity

---

# Business Rules

- Every Prediction History record belongs to exactly one Prediction.
- Every Prediction History record belongs to exactly one Student Snapshot.
- Prediction History records are immutable.
- Every newly generated Prediction automatically creates one Prediction History record.
- Historical predictions are never updated or deleted.

- Prediction History is append-only.
- Historical records are immutable.
- Historical records always reflect the Prediction generated at that time.

# Notes

Prediction History provides a complete audit trail of AI predictions.

Example

```
August Snapshot

↓

Prediction

HIGH

↓

History
```

```
September Snapshot

↓

Prediction

MEDIUM

↓

History
```

Historical records enable:

- Prediction trend analysis
- Model performance evaluation
- AI auditing
- Institutional reporting

Prediction History never replaces the current Prediction.

The `predictions` table always represents the latest prediction.
