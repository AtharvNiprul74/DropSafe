## 📖 Overview

**DropSafe** is an AI-powered platform designed to identify students at risk of dropping out before it becomes too late. By leveraging **Machine Learning**, **predictive analytics**, and a **WhatsApp-based chatbot**, the system enables mentors to take timely action through personalized counseling and academic support.

The platform combines student academic performance, attendance, engagement, and behavioral indicators to generate an accurate risk score, helping educational institutions improve student retention.
